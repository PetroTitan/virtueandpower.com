## Career

Marcus Antonius served under Caesar in Gaul, commanded the left wing at
<a href="/warfare/battles/pharsalus">Pharsalus</a> in 48 BCE, and was
consul in 44 when Caesar was assassinated. His funeral speech and his
handling of the aftermath made him the leading Caesarian.

With Octavian and Lepidus he formed the Second Triumvirate in 43 BCE, a
formally constituted commission with the power to reorder the state. The
proscriptions that followed killed several hundred senators and equestrians,
Cicero among them. At Philippi in 42 BCE the triumvirs defeated the
assassins.

In the division of responsibilities Antony took the east. He formed a
political and personal alliance with Cleopatra VII of Egypt, campaigned
against Parthia with heavy losses, and reorganised the eastern provinces
and client kingdoms.

## Actium

Relations with Octavian broke down through the 30s BCE in a propaganda war
Antony lost decisively. Octavian obtained and published a document he
presented as Antony's will, and framed the coming conflict as a war
against a foreign queen rather than a civil war between Romans.

At <a href="/warfare/battles/actium">Actium</a> in 31 BCE Antony's fleet was
blockaded and broke out with partial success. His army surrendered within
days. He and Cleopatra died in Alexandria the following year.

## The reputation, and who wrote it

Almost everything about Antony reaches us through the tradition the winner
established. The portrait of a capable soldier ruined by drink and by an
Egyptian queen is Augustan, and it was elaborated by poets writing under
Augustus's patronage.

Plutarch's Life, written a century and a half later, is the fullest account
and is more sympathetic than the Augustan version while still working
within its moral frame. Cassius Dio is later still.

This does not mean the charges are false. It means the evidence is a
prosecution case, and the platform reads it as one. The specific claims
that can be checked against other evidence — the Parthian losses, the
territorial arrangements in the east, the political settlement with
Cleopatra — indicate a competent administrator making a coherent if
unsuccessful bid for power, not a man undone by infatuation.
